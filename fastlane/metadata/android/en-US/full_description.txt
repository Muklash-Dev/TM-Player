TM Player (Turbo Media Player) is a free, open-source and completely ad-free video and music player for Android with a built-in web browser, developed by MD. Muklash Mia.

Videos and music are scanned across ALL storage volumes — internal storage, SD card and USB pendrives (OTG).

TM Browser (new in v0.2):
• Tabs and incognito tabs, bookmarks and history grouped by day
• Ad and popup blocking with a 30,000-domain blocklist and optional ad-blocking DNS
• Download manager: live progress and speed, pause / resume, background downloads with notifications, and a download folder you can change (SD card supported)
• Page media finder — play or download the videos, images and files a page offers
• Saved pages for offline reading, desktop / mobile mode, night mode, text size, orientation lock
• Search engines: Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing and more, plus your own custom engines

Player features:
• PLAYit-style home: list or grid view, month grouping, sort by date, name, size or duration, watch history with one-tap clear
• Folders, playlists and search across every volume
• Gesture player: brightness / volume / seek swipes, double-tap skip (5–30 s), long-press speed boost, playback speeds 0.25x–4.0x
• Screenshot button, HDR Enhance colour boost, picture-in-picture, background play, sleep timer
• Music player with albums, artists, folders, playlists and a mini player
• Private Vault: PIN + fingerprint lock for your videos, hidden from the gallery
• Recycle Bin, Media Manage storage overview and a junk Clean page
• Video to MP3/audio converter
• Themes: dark/light, six accent colours and your own photo theme
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts. The player works fully offline; the browser only connects to the websites you open

TM Player is an original work built on Android's public media APIs. Playback uses your device's built-in codecs. Works on Android 8–17.
